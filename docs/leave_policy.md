# Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
- Full-time employees receive 20 vacation days per year
- Part-time employees receive pro-rated vacation days
- Vacation must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance

## Sick Leave
- 10 sick days per year
- Medical certificate required for absences over 3 days
- Notify manager within 24 hours of absence

## Parental Leave
- Maternity leave: 12 weeks paid
- Paternity leave: 4 weeks paid
- Adoption leave: 6 weeks paid

## Special Leave
- Bereavement: 5 days
- Jury duty: Up to 10 days with documentation
- Personal days: 3 days per year for emergencies
