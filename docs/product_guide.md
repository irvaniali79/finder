# Product Guide - ProCRM

## Overview
ProCRM is our flagship customer relationship management platform designed for small to medium businesses.

## Key Features
- Contact management with custom fields
- Sales pipeline tracking with deal stages
- Email integration and templates
- Reporting dashboard with analytics
- Mobile app for iOS and Android

## Getting Started
1. Sign up at our website
2. Import contacts via CSV or manual entry
3. Set up your sales pipeline stages
4. Configure email templates
5. Invite team members

## Pricing
- Starter: $29/month (up to 5 users)
- Professional: $79/month (up to 20 users)
- Enterprise: $199/month (unlimited users)

## Support
- Live chat: Available in-app during business hours
- Knowledge base: docs.procrm.com
- Training sessions: Book via support portal
